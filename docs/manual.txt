# Intro
PurrOne is a two oscillator mono synth in the spirit of the classic Pro-One class of analog synths: two oscillators with sync, a sub oscillator, a four pole filter that can sing on its own, two envelopes, an LFO and a small modulation matrix. It adds three colour stages (Drive, Transistor, Fuzz), a 3 band EQ, a short reverb and a gentle maximizer with a safety limiter. Made for bass, leads and acid lines.

# Quick start
- Pick a factory preset, hold a note and move Cutoff and Reso first.
- Raise the Sub knob in the Mixer for weight under Osc A.
- Colour: Drive is a clean symmetric clip, Transistor is the warm Neve-style stage (even harmonics, a little low-end weight), Fuzz is the hard one. They work in series and can be mixed.
- Watch the level: Drive, Transistor and Fuzz are level matched, so compare by ear with the knob at zero and at full.
- Save your own sounds with Save; they stay on your computer when you uninstall unless you choose to remove them.

# Recommendations
- Sub bass: Mixer Sub 80 to 95 percent, Osc A and B low, Cutoff around 400 to 700 Hz, Transistor 5 to 15 percent.
- Reese bass: both oscillators on Saw, Tune B up a little (about 0.1), Transistor 40 to 60 percent, slow LFO on the filter.
- Acid line: Reso 70 to 85 percent, short filter decay, Auto glide on.
- Leads: Cutoff 6 to 12 kHz, a little Space, lower the EQ High a few dB if it gets harsh.
- If a sound feels thin, try Transistor before Drive: it adds warmth without much brightness.

# Install
- Windows: run PurrOne-Setup.exe. To remove it use Settings, Apps, PurrOne. The uninstaller asks whether to also delete your saved presets.
- macOS: open PurrOne-macOS.pkg. To remove it run "Uninstall PurrOne" from the Applications folder; it asks whether to also delete your saved presets.
- Linux (Flatpak DAW): flatpak install --user the .flatpak file; remove with flatpak uninstall. Linux (native DAW): extract the tarball into ~/.vst3 and delete the PurrOne.vst3 folder to remove.
- Your saved presets live in a folder named Studio 87/PurrOne/presets in your user application data folder (details in INSTALL.txt).
