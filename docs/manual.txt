# Intro
PurrOne is a two oscillator mono synth in the spirit of the classic Pro-One class of analog synths: two oscillators with sync, a sub oscillator, a four pole filter that can sing on its own, two envelopes, an LFO and a small modulation matrix. It adds three colour stages (Drive, Transistor, Fuzz), a 3 band EQ, a short reverb and a gentle maximizer with a safety limiter. Made for bass, leads and acid lines.
- Filter modes: LP keeps the lows, HP keeps the highs, BP keeps a band around the cutoff (nasal, talking sounds).

# Quick start
- Pick a factory preset, hold a note and move Cutoff and Reso first.
- Raise the Sub knob in the Mixer for weight under Osc A.
- Colour: Drive is a clean symmetric clip, Transistor is the warm Neve-style stage (even harmonics, a little low-end weight), Fuzz is the hard one. They work in series and can be mixed. The PRE | POST | BOTH switch in the Filter header sets where they sit: PRE puts them before the filter (the filter shapes the grit), POST after it (the grit stays bright), BOTH is a light stage before and the full stage after.
- Watch the level: Drive, Transistor and Fuzz are level matched, so compare by ear with the knob at zero and at full.
- Save your own sounds with Save; they stay on your computer when you uninstall unless you choose to remove them.

- Unison: the UNISON buttons (1 to 5) stack detuned copies of both oscillators through the one filter; Detune sets the spread, up to 100 cents. 1 is off. With Sync on, only oscillator B is stacked.

# Pages
The tabs in the header switch between Sound, Seq / Arp, FX and Settings. Info bubbles explain every control.

# Seq / Arp
- Mode: Off plays your keys as usual, Seq plays the step sequence, Arp plays the held keys one at a time.
- Steps: 1 to 64 per pattern, 4 patterns (A to D). Click a note to raise it, Shift+click or the wheel lowers it; Gate, Accent and Slide are on or off per step. Slide glides in from the step before. Chance (100, 75, 50 or 25 %) and Ratchet (1 to 4 hits per step) cycle on click, Shift+click goes back. A step that chance drops plays nothing, and a slide never waits for a dropped note.
- Start: With host (the DAW transport starts it and its tempo is followed), on key (pressing a key starts it and the lowest held key transposes the pattern), or a chosen start note (that note toggles it and is not played). The Play button starts it by hand. Mode Off turns it all off.
- 303 squelch (off by default): accents and slides push the resonance for a moment. Accent loudness and brightness always work: an accented step jumps in level and opens the filter, scaled by the Accent knob.
- Sequence presets are small files in Studio 87/PurrOne/sequences. Share gives a text code, Import reads one back.
- MIDI out is off by default. When on, the sequence or arp also sends its notes out so it can play another synth.

# FX
Signal order is fixed: Colour, Filter, Amp, Crush, EQ, Space, Opto, OTT, Dance master, Maximizer, Stereo, Limiter. New modules start off or neutral.
- EQ: five bands. Drag a point, wheel over it changes Q. Each band can act on the stereo signal, the mid or the side, and can be dynamic. Dynamic: positive values boost, negative values cut, by up to that many dB when the band is above its threshold. The graph shows the threshold line, the live level in the band, what the band is doing now (solid dot) and where it ends up at full effect (hollow dot, dashed curve).
- Space: pre-delay, damping, width and ducking (lowers the tail while you play).
- Crush starts at 8 bit, 12 kHz, 70% mix when you switch it on. Opto speed, OTT, Dance master (3 band compressor and clipper), Maximizer and the limiter ceiling are on the Dynamics page. Stereo has width, mono below, mid and side level and drive.
- Every module has a Presets button in the left rail; a module preset changes only that module.
- The analyzer (spectrum, scope, phase) reads the final output.

# Settings
Banks A to C hold the factory sounds and are read only. Banks D to G are yours: the one selected is where Save puts new sounds. Banks and single sounds can be exported and imported as files. Tuning has Equal, Just, Pythagorean and Custom (drag the bars) plus A4 at 432, 440 or 442. Quality sets 4x or 8x oversampling. Random LFO, pitch wheel range (2, 5, 12 or 24 semitones), wheel CC and window size are here too. The two wheels left of the on-screen keyboard are the pitch wheel (it springs back to the middle; the range is set on the Settings page) and the mod wheel (the same control as the Wheel knob; double-click to zero it). A MIDI keyboard moves them too. Under MIDI In, the Mod wheel and Aftertouch knobs send each to filter cutoff (bipolar, in octaves), vibrato (uses the LFO rate) and extra reverb.

MIDI learn: right-click any knob or switch, choose MIDI learn, then move a control on your controller. Right-click again to forget it or reset the control. Mappings are saved with the DAW project.

Sound browser: the dice by the sound name picks a random bank A sound and nudges cutoff, resonance and envelope a little; the star adds or removes the sound from your favourites (a text file next to your sounds). The list has a Favourites menu and each bank is grouped by category.

# Recommendations
- Sub bass: Mixer Sub 80 to 95 percent, Osc A and B low, Cutoff around 400 to 700 Hz, Transistor 5 to 15 percent.
- Reese bass: both oscillators on Saw, Tune B up a little (about 0.1), Transistor 40 to 60 percent, slow LFO on the filter.
- Acid line: Reso 70 to 85 percent, short filter decay, Auto glide on.
- Leads: Cutoff 6 to 12 kHz, a little Space, lower the EQ High a few dB if it gets harsh.
- If a sound feels thin, try Transistor before Drive: it adds warmth without much brightness.

# Install
- Windows: run PurrOne-Setup.exe. To remove it use Settings, Apps, PurrOne. The uninstaller asks whether to also delete your saved presets.
- macOS: open PurrOne-macOS.pkg. To remove it run "Uninstall PurrOne" from the Applications folder; it asks whether to also delete your saved presets.
- Linux (Flatpak DAW): flatpak install --user the .flatpak file; remove with flatpak uninstall. Linux (native DAW): extract the tarball into ~/.vst3 and delete the PurrOne.vst3 folder to remove.
- Your saved presets live in a folder named Studio 87/PurrOne/presets in your user application data folder (details in INSTALL.txt).
